## A. EPISODE SYNOPSIS

**Logline:** On an ordinary golden afternoon, a tired engineer and a woman with a handwritten bookmark share one accidental glance in a hillside café, and neither of them knows it is the first page of their story.

**Synopsis:**
It is 4:30 PM at **Café Liwayway**, a quiet hillside café with tall west-facing windows edged with magenta bougainvillea. **Renzo**, a hardworking site engineer, stops in for a cup of coffee before another overtime shift. He takes his usual window seat, opens a worn sketchbook full of buildings he can't afford to build yet, and gets a text from his coworker Jeric: *OT ulit mamaya.*

A few tables away, **Rose** reads a sage-green hardcover novel. Their eyes meet by accident. Time slows down. She gives a small smile; he looks away, then looks back.

When Rose gets up for her order, a handmade bookmark slips out of her book: a pressed bougainvillea flower with *"Sa tamang panahon"* written in her grandmother's cursive. It lands by Renzo's table. Both of them reach for it, and their hands almost touch. He gives it back. They share their first smiles and their first words.

On her way back, Rose notices his sketchbook. A short, shy conversation follows: a dream, a laugh, and two first names. Then a text from her friend Trish pulls her away. At the door she looks back and smiles.

Renzo smiles to himself, then finds the bookmark on his table, half-hidden under his sketchbook. Outside, Rose opens her book, sees that the bookmark is gone, and smiles **as if she knows exactly where she left it.**

Was it an accident, or did she leave it on purpose? Episode 1 ends on that question.

**Episode themes:** chance, timing, quiet hope. *Some beginnings don't announce themselves.*

## D. AI VIDEO PRODUCTION PROMPT PACK

### D1. Recommended workflow (for consistent faces)
1. **Lock the keyframes first.** For each shot, generate a still frame with an image model that supports character reference, attaching the **Rose** and/or **Renzo** photo. Approve the faces before you animate anything.
2. **Animate shot by shot.** Most AI video generators produce clips of about 5–10 seconds. Use **image-to-video** from each approved keyframe with the short shot prompts below, rather than trying to generate a full 30-second scene in one pass.
3. **Re-attach both references on every generation**, and paste the identity lock blocks every time. Reuse the same seed and style settings across a scene where your tool allows it.
4. **Generate dialogue audio separately** (TTS or real voice actors), then apply a lip-sync pass on the dialogue close-ups.
5. **Add all text in post:** phone messages, the bookmark cursive, subtitles and the end card.
6. **Color-match** all clips in your editor with one golden-hour grade so AI variations blend together.

## H. EPISODE ENDING & CLIFFHANGER

**Ending beat:** Renzo, alone at golden hour, holds the bookmark that says *"Sa tamang panahon"* up to the setting sun. The narration lands, and the screen fades to black.

**The cliffhanger question:** Rose sees that her bookmark is missing and **smiles instead of turning back.** So:
> **Did she leave it by accident, or on purpose?**
> And now Renzo has something that belongs to her. **Will he ever see her again to return it?**

This hook drives Episode 2. It leaves viewers wanting the next meeting without rushing the romance.
